# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Convert WAV files to MP3 on this computer, using local ffmpeg.
- Open in English, and switch to German from the header. The choice is remembered.
- Choose 128, 192, 256, or 320 kbps. 320 is the default.
- Download one file, or save every finished MP3 in a single ZIP.
- Keep the work on localhost. Nothing is uploaded.
